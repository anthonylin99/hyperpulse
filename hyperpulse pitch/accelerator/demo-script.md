# HyperPulse Demo Script

## Positioning

HyperPulse is a read-only, Hyperliquid-native intelligence workspace for active perpetual futures traders. The product helps traders move from market context to portfolio review without asking for private keys, seed phrases, approvals, or custody.

## Demo Goal

Show that HyperPulse can become the default daily workspace for active Hyperliquid traders: fast market scanning, asset-level context, read-only wallet review, position performance, and trust-first documentation.

## Core Flow

1. Home
   - Open with the simple promise: read-only Hyperliquid intelligence for active perp traders.
   - Emphasize that public demo surfaces are available without connecting a wallet.
   - Call out the trust posture early: no private keys, no seed phrase, no custody.

2. Markets
   - Move from the homepage into the markets table.
   - Show how a trader can scan listed perpetual markets quickly.
   - Frame this as the daily starting point: what is moving, where attention should go, and which asset deserves deeper review.

3. Asset Drawer And Reaction Levels
   - Open an asset drawer from the markets table.
   - Walk through the asset summary, current market context, and reaction levels.
   - Position reaction levels as decision-support context, not trade execution or financial advice.

4. Read-Only Portfolio
   - Paste a public wallet address into the portfolio view.
   - State clearly: this is read-only wallet lookup using public data.
   - Repeat the safety line: HyperPulse never needs a private key, seed phrase, trading approval, or custody permission.

5. Positions, Performance, And Journal
   - Review open positions and recent performance.
   - Show how a trader can connect market context with portfolio outcomes.
   - Highlight the journal as a lightweight review layer for improving trading discipline.

6. Docs And Trust
   - End in Docs.
   - Show the product explains what data is used, what permissions are not required, and how the read-only model works.
   - Close on trust: HyperPulse is designed as an intelligence workspace, not a wallet, broker, exchange, or custodian.

## Talk Track

"HyperPulse gives active Hyperliquid perp traders one workspace to understand markets, review assets, and inspect portfolio performance. The key design choice is that the product is read-only. A trader can browse markets and paste a public wallet address without ever sharing a private key, seed phrase, approval, or custody permission. The demo moves from market discovery to asset context, then into read-only portfolio review and documentation so users can trust what the product does and does not do."

## Demo Guardrails

- Keep the demo focused on the public Home, Markets, Portfolio, and Docs surfaces.
- Do not imply trade execution, custody, or wallet control.
- Do not ask for or display private keys, seed phrases, or signing prompts.
- Keep language focused on intelligence, context, workflow, and review.
