# HyperPulse Accelerator One-Page Brief

## Company

HyperPulse is a read-only, Hyperliquid-native intelligence workspace for active Hyperliquid perpetuals traders.

## Problem

Hyperliquid traders move quickly, but their workflow is fragmented across market tables, funding screens, public wallet checks, trade tape, order book context, and personal notes. The result is slower reaction time, weaker post-trade review, and too much manual context-switching before acting on volatile perp markets.

## Solution

HyperPulse gives traders a focused public-demo workspace for Hyperliquid market intelligence and read-only wallet review. It is designed to help users understand market context, likely reaction pressure, and portfolio behavior without requiring custody, private keys, seed phrases, or trade execution permissions.

## Current Public Demo Surfaces

- Home: product positioning, navigation, and onboarding context.
- Markets: table-first Hyperliquid market directory with funding, tape context, and Reaction Map setup/status.
- Portfolio: read-only wallet review with performance, positions, and journal.
- Docs: product explanation, usage context, and safety framing.

Public demo surfaces intentionally exclude hidden or retired trading, factor, and whale features.

## Differentiation

- Hyperliquid-native: built around the workflows and signals active Hyperliquid perp traders already care about.
- Read-only by design: browser wallet/public address analytics only; no private keys, no seed phrase, no custody, and no transaction signing requirement.
- Table-first markets workflow: fast scanning for funding, market context, and tape-aware conditions instead of a generic dashboard.
- Reaction Map: currently covers BTC, ETH, and SOL using public Hyperliquid trades, open interest changes, book depth, and funding.
- Honest signal framing: Reaction Map should be understood as likely reaction pressure, not exact exchange-wide trader positions or guaranteed liquidation levels.

## Product Status

HyperPulse has a public demo structure across Home, Markets, Portfolio, and Docs. The strongest wedge today is a safer intelligence layer for active Hyperliquid perp traders who want market context and wallet review without handing over control of assets.

## Ideal Accelerator Fit

HyperPulse is best suited for crypto-native accelerators that can help with user distribution, trader communities, market-structure feedback, data partnerships, and fundraising narrative. The near-term accelerator goal is to sharpen the product wedge, validate retention with active Hyperliquid traders, and convert demo usage into a credible seed-stage story.

## Accelerator Ask

We are seeking accelerator support across:

- Crypto-native go-to-market and community distribution.
- Perp trader discovery, interviews, and beta cohorts.
- Data and infrastructure mentorship for reliable market intelligence.
- Fundraising narrative, investor introductions, and demo-day readiness.

## Core Message

HyperPulse is not another trading bot, broker, or execution layer. It is a read-only intelligence workspace for Hyperliquid perp traders, built to turn public market data and wallet review into faster, safer, more structured decision support.
