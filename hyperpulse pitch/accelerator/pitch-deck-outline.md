# HyperPulse Accelerator Deck Outline

## 1. Title

**HyperPulse: the read-only intelligence workspace for Hyperliquid perp traders**

- Hyperliquid-native market structure, portfolio review, and journal context.
- Built for active perp traders who need faster context without handing over control.
- Trust posture: no custody, no private keys, no seed phrases, no transaction signing.

## 2. Problem

Active Hyperliquid traders are forced to stitch together too many tabs before they can make sense of a market.

- Market tables, funding, tape, order book context, public wallet review, and notes are fragmented.
- Volatility compresses reaction time.
- Existing dashboards either stay generic or drift toward execution and custody risk.

## 3. Solution / Demo Workflow

HyperPulse turns the public demo into a focused pre-trade and post-trade intelligence loop.

- Home frames the workspace and safety boundary.
- Markets gives table-first scanning for funding, tape context, and Reaction Map setup/status.
- Portfolio gives read-only wallet review with performance, positions, and journal.
- Docs explains signal methodology and product boundaries.

## 4. Wedge

The wedge is not another execution venue. It is the safer intelligence layer around Hyperliquid behavior.

- Hyperliquid-native market structure.
- Read-only portfolio and journal workflow.
- Reaction Map levels show likely reaction pressure from public Hyperliquid data, not exact exchange-wide liquidations.
- No execution surface, no factor model, no whale surveillance claim in the pitch.

## 5. Market

Perp traders are increasingly protocol-native, faster moving, and underserved by generic analytics.

- Hyperliquid concentrates active onchain perp behavior into a public, data-rich venue.
- The first customer is the serious active trader who already checks funding, OI, tape, depth, and wallet performance.
- Expansion comes from teams, communities, and advanced traders who want repeatable review workflows.

## 6. Product

The product is a terminal-style workspace that makes trader context scannable.

- Market directory for fast triage.
- Reaction Map for pressure zones on BTC, ETH, and SOL using public trades, OI changes, book depth, and funding.
- Portfolio review for read-only wallet analytics.
- Journal layer to connect market context to trader behavior.

## 7. Traction / Beta Plan

The current milestone is converting a public demo into a measurable beta loop.

- Public demo surfaces exist across Home, Markets, Portfolio, and Docs.
- Recruit active Hyperliquid traders for weekly beta cohorts.
- Measure activation, repeat market scans, portfolio review frequency, journal completion, and interview-backed retention.
- Use beta feedback to refine signal clarity and workflow speed.

## 8. Business Model

HyperPulse starts with software revenue, not execution take-rate dependency.

- Free public demo for discovery and education.
- Pro subscription for active traders who need persistent watchlists, deeper portfolio history, saved journal workflows, and expanded signal context.
- Team/community plans for trader groups, research desks, and educator-led cohorts.
- Future data/API packaging only after signal quality and trust boundaries are proven.

## 9. Roadmap

The roadmap sharpens the Hyperliquid-native intelligence wedge before broadening.

- Near term: beta cohorts, signal explainability, journal templates, wallet import polish.
- Mid term: cohort dashboards, alerting around watchlist context, richer post-trade review.
- Later: expanded market coverage, data exports, and team workflows.
- Guardrail: keep the product non-custodial and read-only.

## 10. Ask

HyperPulse is seeking accelerator support to turn a focused wedge into a seed-ready company.

- Distribution into crypto-native trader communities.
- Perp market-structure mentorship and data infrastructure feedback.
- Beta cohort access for customer discovery.
- Fundraising narrative support and investor introductions.
