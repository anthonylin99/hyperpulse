## Messaging Guardrails

- Describe HyperPulse as read-only intelligence, not a trading bot, broker, or execution platform.
- Say Reaction Map shows likely reaction pressure using public Hyperliquid trades, OI changes, book depth, and funding.
- Do not describe Reaction Map as exact exchange-wide positions, guaranteed liquidation levels, or full-market trader intent.
- Keep public-demo claims limited to Home, Markets, Portfolio, and Docs.
- Note that trading, factors, and whale surfaces are hidden or retired from the public demo.
- Reinforce that HyperPulse never asks for private keys or seed phrases and uses browser wallet/public address analytics only.

## Short Application Blurb

HyperPulse is a read-only, Hyperliquid-native intelligence workspace for active perpetuals traders. The public demo includes Home, Markets, Portfolio, and Docs. Markets provides a table-first Hyperliquid directory with funding, tape context, and Reaction Map setup/status; Portfolio gives read-only wallet review with performance, positions, and journal. Reaction Map currently covers BTC, ETH, and SOL using public Hyperliquid trades, open interest changes, book depth, and funding to estimate likely reaction pressure, not exact exchange-wide positions. HyperPulse requires no private keys or seed phrase and is designed as a safer intelligence layer before traders act.
