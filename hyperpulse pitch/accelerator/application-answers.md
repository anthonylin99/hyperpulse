# Accelerator Application Answers

## Reusable Company Description

HyperPulse is a read-only, Hyperliquid-native intelligence workspace for active perpetual futures traders. It brings market scanning, asset-level context, public wallet portfolio review, performance tracking, and trading journal workflows into one trust-first product without requiring private keys, seed phrases, approvals, or custody.

## Problem

Active perp traders move across fragmented tools to understand market activity, inspect positions, and review performance. That workflow is slow, error-prone, and often mixes research, portfolio tracking, and execution in ways that reduce trust. HyperPulse gives traders a dedicated intelligence workspace built around public Hyperliquid data and read-only wallet lookup.

## Solution

HyperPulse starts with public demo surfaces: Home, Markets, Portfolio, and Docs. A trader can scan markets, open an asset drawer for reaction levels, paste a public wallet address for read-only portfolio analysis, review positions and performance, and use the journal to improve decision quality. The product is deliberately non-custodial and does not request private keys, seed phrases, or trading permissions.

## Why Now

Hyperliquid has become a core venue for on-chain perpetual futures trading, but trader tooling has not caught up with the speed, depth, and specialization of the market. As more active traders operate on Hyperliquid, they need native intelligence workflows that are fast, transparent, and trust-first.

## Users

The initial user is an active Hyperliquid perp trader who checks markets frequently, manages multiple positions, reviews performance, and wants better context before and after trades. The product is especially useful for traders who want a single workspace without giving up wallet control.

## Traction

The initial public demo focuses on four surfaces: Home, Markets, Portfolio, and Docs. The launch path is to drive beta users through a simple flow: homepage, markets table, asset drawer and reaction levels, read-only public wallet portfolio, positions and performance, journal, then docs and trust.

## Defensibility

HyperPulse is differentiated by focus: Hyperliquid-native workflows, active perp trader use cases, and a trust-first read-only architecture. The wedge is becoming the daily intelligence layer traders use before and after execution, rather than trying to become an exchange, wallet, or custodian.

## Business Model

HyperPulse can monetize through a freemium product with paid tiers for advanced portfolio analytics, saved workspaces, deeper performance review, and team or professional trader workflows. The free public demo builds trust before asking users to pay.
